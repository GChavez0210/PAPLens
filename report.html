How key metrics are calculated

Average AHI
sum(nightly AHI) / nights
Tracks residual sleep-disordered breathing burden.
Adherence / CMS Compliance
nights ≥ 4 hrs / total nights × 100
CMS requires ≥ 70% of nights with ≥ 4 hrs.
Leak 95th Percentile
P95 of nightly peak leak values
Values ≥ 24 L/min reduce pressure accuracy.
RIN (Respiratory Disturbance)
flow-limited breaths not scored / hr
Sub-threshold resistance that may fragment sleep.
Snore Index
% of epochs with snore amplitude > 0.1
Persistent snoring may signal sub-optimal pressure.
Pressure Efficiency / r
% epochs ≥ 90% max | Pearson r
High ceiling usage may indicate a constrained max setting.
