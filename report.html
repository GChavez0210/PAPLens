How key metrics are calculated

Average AHI
sum(nightly AHI) / number of nights
Clinical value: tracks residual sleep-disordered breathing burden under therapy.
Adherence Rate / 30-Day CMS Compliance
nights ≥ 4 hrs / nights with recorded usage × 100
Clinical value: CMS requires ≥ 70% of nights with ≥ 4 hrs to classify therapy as compliant for coverage.
Leak 95th Percentile
95th percentile of nightly peak leak values
Clinical value: values ≥ 24 L/min can reduce delivered pressure and event detection accuracy.
Respiratory Disturbance Index (RIN)
flow-limited breaths not classified as apneas or hypopneas / hr
Clinical value: captures sub-threshold upper airway resistance that may fragment sleep without scoring in AHI.
Snore Index
% of 2-second epochs where snore amplitude > 0.1
Clinical value: persistent snoring on therapy may indicate sub-optimal pressure or mouth breathing leak.
Pressure Efficiency / Correlation r
% epochs ≥ 90% of session max pressure | Pearson r from -1.00 to +1.00
High efficiency near ceiling suggests the max setting may be constraining the device. Correlation shows co-movement, not causation.

Bring these questions to your clinician

  1. Are my residual events mostly obstructive, central, or mixed based on this event profile and trend charts?
  2. If leak is elevated, should we adjust mask type/fit before pressure setting changes?
  3. Does my pressure pattern look reactive (events first, pressure second) or insufficient baseline support?
  4. If RIN is elevated relative to AHI, could upper airway resistance or flow limitation be causing sleep fragmentation not captured by the event index?
  5. Given adherence, compliance rate, and symptom history, what is the most useful next intervention before my next follow-up?
